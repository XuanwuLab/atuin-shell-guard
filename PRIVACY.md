## 1. Collection and Use of Personal Information

During your use of the Service, we will adhere to the principles of lawfulness, legitimacy, minimum necessity, openness, and transparency, and will collect and process information that you actively provide or that is generated through your use of the Service in the manner described below, in order to provide you with the Service, optimize our Service, and safeguard the security of your Service. **The Service currently does not require or provide account registration or login functionality.**

### 1.1 Cloud-Based Risk Assessment

When you enable this plugin and the Agent platform attempts to execute a tool invocation, **we need to collect the tool-invocation command that the Agent is about to execute, the current working directory, and the necessary execution context**, in order to predict the files, directories, or other resources that may be affected by the Agent's tool-invocation behavior. In addition, **we need to collect necessary metadata of the affected resources (such as file name, file size, creation time, and modification time)** in order to determine the risk level of the affected files and provide you with the risk-assessment result. When the local plugin matches a potentially risky behavior pattern (such as deleting a large number of files or deleting files with specific extensions), **the tool-invocation command that the Agent is about to execute, the necessary metadata of the affected resources, the randomly generated installation ID, conversation ID, and other telemetry data (including the type of Agent platform currently in use, performance profiling data such as script execution time, and fault-reporting data such as crash reports) will be uploaded to the cloud for risk assessment**, and the cloud-recommended handling approach will be returned to the Agent. **Please note that the contents of your local files will not be uploaded to the cloud.**

If you do not wish to use the cloud-based risk assessment feature, you may at any time disable it as described in the README documentation or uninstall the plugin.

### 1.2 Feedback

If you have any feedback, criticism, or suggestions regarding our Service, you may submit your comments by email (see Section 10 for details). If you voluntarily provide personal information in your email (for example, an email signature, contact information, or screenshots), such information is provided and controlled by you, and we will use it solely for the purpose of handling and responding to your feedback.
